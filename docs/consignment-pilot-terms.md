# RESTYLE commission & consignment framework — PILOT DRAFT

**Status:** Internal proposal only. Not legal advice. Not approved for live consignments.

## Proposed tier schedule

| Realized selling price | Consignor share of net proceeds | RESTYLE share of net proceeds |
|---|---:|---:|
| $100–$499 | 60% | 40% |
| $500–$999 | 70% | 30% |
| $1,000+ | 80% | 20% |

**Default intake:** Items expected under $100 normally declined. Thresholds are initial RESTYLE proposals.

## Pilot e-sign agreement text

PILOT DRAFT - FOR WORKFLOW TESTING ONLY. NOT A FINAL OR LEGALLY APPROVED CONSIGNMENT AGREEMENT. NO PHYSICAL MERCHANDISE MAY BE SHIPPED UNDER THIS PILOT.

1. Parties and property. The item owner ("Consignor") states they own the submitted merchandise, have authority to consign it, and have accurately disclosed its brand, authenticity, condition and any defects. RESTYLE may decline merchandise following physical inspection. Title remains with Consignor until a completed sale unless the final signed terms state otherwise.

2. Preliminary approval. Approval based on photos is provisional. Do not send merchandise until RESTYLE issues final signed-agreement shipping instructions. Items below an expected $100 resale value are generally not accepted.

3. Payout tiers. For an item's final realized selling price of $100-$499, Consignor receives 60% of Net Sale Proceeds; $500-$999, 70%; $1,000 or more, 80%. RESTYLE retains the remaining 40%, 30%, or 20%, respectively. The tier is determined by the actual final selling price (excluding separately collected sales tax). The illustrated ranges are pilot defaults requiring final confirmation.

4. Net Sale Proceeds means amounts RESTYLE actually collects from the sale, excluding sales tax, less the actual applicable marketplace/payment fees, shipping and insurance costs not separately covered by the buyer, and eligible documented transaction-specific costs disclosed before consignment. No undisclosed general overhead or labor charge is deducted. Returns and refunds reduce or reverse the sale; double deductions are prohibited. RESTYLE provides a basic settlement statement.

5. Shipping and returns costs. Consignor pays for tracked and appropriately insured shipping of accepted merchandise to RESTYLE and, if unsold, return shipping back to Consignor. RESTYLE remains the seller of record and handles all marketplace obligations to buyers. Where permitted by law and the final signed terms, actual documented buyer-return shipping, refunds and dispute costs attributable to the consigned item may reduce sale proceeds; RESTYLE remains responsible for losses directly resulting from its own negligence, inaccurate listings or mishandling. Any sale reversed by refund has no distributable proceeds until the item is resold or the matter otherwise settles.

6. Minimum term. Accepted merchandise is consigned for a minimum of 90 calendar days beginning when its first public marketplace listing goes live. Early withdrawal requires RESTYLE's written consent and may involve only reasonable, previously disclosed direct costs. RESTYLE will contact Consignor about any proposed material price reduction; no automatic markdown is authorized by this draft.

7. Payment schedule. On a successfully completed sale, payout will be scheduled no earlier than 30 calendar days after confirmed delivery to the buyer, and only after sale funds are collected and any returns, chargebacks, fraud claims or platform disputes are resolved. If a claim remains open, payment on that item is postponed until resolution and an updated statement is provided. The 30-day period is not a guarantee that funds become available during an open dispute. Any payout method and legal withholding rights require final agreement.

8. End of term and unsold goods. After the 90-day minimum, RESTYLE and Consignor will agree whether to renew, adjust the asking price or arrange return. No donation, abandonment, disposal or ongoing storage fee is permitted under this draft without separate written authorization.

9. Documentation. RESTYLE may securely store item photos, assessment notes, shipping/tracking details, transaction records and signed agreement. Electronic-signature consent is requested separately. This draft is for internal pilot testing and is not an offer to accept goods.

10. Governing requirements. The final agreement, before actual consignment, must be reviewed for applicable Florida secondhand-dealer, e-signature, consumer-protection, tax, insurance, and marketplace requirements. Nothing here overrides mandatory buyer protection rights or applicable law.

## Pilot-only shipping instructions

RESTYLE PILOT - DO NOT SHIP ANY PHYSICAL ITEMS. This is a workflow demonstration only. The live shipping address, package preparation instructions, insurance requirement and inbound shipment reference will be supplied after legal and operational approval. Proposed future procedure: consignor pays for tracked and appropriately insured inbound shipping and includes the RESTYLE submission reference. Do not send additional or unapproved items.

## Operational gates before real customers

- Confirm tier thresholds and fee definition, including fee base and buyer-paid shipping.
- Review consignment registration, identity and merchandise hold requirements with Florida counsel.
- Approve a physical return address, shipment liability/insurance, return reasons and dispute allocation.
- Settle payout accounting and post-payout refunds/chargebacks policies; 30 days after confirmed delivery is a target when no open dispute.
- Provide legally reviewed terms and actual warehouse shipping instructions; disable pilot mode only after counsel review.
- Configure verified sending domain and transactional email delivery, confirm approved email wording before live sending.
