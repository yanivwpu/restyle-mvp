Install SHUKCHIC on your phone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). On newer iPhones, tap the Page Menu first, then Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen and confirm Open as Web App, if shown.
  4. Tap Add, then open the SHUKCHIC icon from your Home Screen to register your boutique.
  1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the browser's ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm installation, then tap the SHUKCHIC icon from your Home Screen to set up your store.
On your phone, visit shukchic.com/merchant in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). Use the browser menu to add it to your Home Screen.

Registration begins after you open the installed app icon. You won't need to fill out a website application first.
