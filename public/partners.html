START FROM YOUR PHONE

Your SHUKCHIC store starts in the app.

Install the merchant app, create your boutique account and enter your store details from your phone. We'll review your registration. Once approved, sign your individual partnership agreement in the app and begin sending items for review.

Android: choose Install app or Add to Home screen in your browser. iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

MERCHANT EARNINGS

How and when merchants get paid

Eligible merchants may keep up to 80% of net sale proceeds. Your exact share, deductions and payout timing are set in the partnership agreement you review and sign inside the app. Payouts depend on verified sales, cleared marketplace funds, delivery and any applicable returns or disputes. The earnings shown in the app are estimates until SHUKCHIC reconciles the sale.

Get the Merchant App to review your individual terms after approval.
